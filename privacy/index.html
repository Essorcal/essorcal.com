ReStaq

Privacy

Effective 2026-08-20

ReStaq collects no personal data. Nothing about you or your use of the app reaches the developer.

No accounts, no tracking

ReStaq has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The app never asks for your name, email, or anything else about you.

Your collection stays yours

Everything you enter — packets, decks, custom card lists, missing-card flags — is stored on your device. If your device is signed into iCloud, iOS mirrors that data to your own private iCloud database so your devices stay in sync. That storage belongs to your Apple ID; the developer cannot read it. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy govern it, and you can turn iCloud off for ReStaq in iOS Settings at any time.

Card images

The app fetches Magic card imagery and card data from Scryfall when needed. Those requests carry standard network metadata (such as an IP address) and no personal information; images are cached on your device. Scryfall’s own privacy policy governs its service.

NFC stickers

A ReStaq sticker stores only an anonymous packet identifier — a link that points at a card list. No personal information is ever written to a sticker.

This website

essorcal.com is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics; GitHub may keep standard server logs per GitHub’s privacy statement.
